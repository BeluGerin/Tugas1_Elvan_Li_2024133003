Universitas Universal (UVERS) is a private university located in Batam City, Riau Islands, Indonesia. Established in 2014 under the Yayasan Pancaran Maitri (the same foundation behind Maitreyawira schools), UVERS focuses on providing higher education rooted in character building and universal values. Its foundational core philosophy revolves around the concept of "Dunia Satu Keluarga" (One World, One Family), emphasizing a community that embraces humanity across all cultures, backgrounds, and beliefs.
