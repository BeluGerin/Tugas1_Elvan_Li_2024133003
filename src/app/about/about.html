About Us

Foundational Values: UVERS instills three core student values: Antusias (Enthusiasm), Ramah (Friendliness), and Kasih (Compassion).

Global Network: The university is actively expanding its international academic footprint through collaborations with regional institutions, such as Yuan Ze University and South China Normal University. It regularly highlights languages and multicultural studies, notably through regional initiatives like the Chinese Bridge language competition.

Modern Facilities: UVERS recently inaugurated its new building facilities at its Pasir Putih campus to accommodate growing student bodies and academic pursuits.
